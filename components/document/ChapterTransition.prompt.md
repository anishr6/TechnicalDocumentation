Closes a chapter and points at the next one. Sits after the chapter's KeyTakeaway and before the next h2.

```jsx
<ChapterTransition next="Assignment and Escalation" />
<ChapterTransition label="Then">We turn to the escalation matrix and its owners.</ChapterTransition>
```

One line only. State what comes next, never why it matters. Do not use one between
sub-sections (h3) — only between chapters.
