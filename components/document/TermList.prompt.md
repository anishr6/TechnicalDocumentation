The workhorse list of a technical document: plain bullets, or "**Term:** explanation" pairs.

```jsx
<TermList items={[
  { term: "Encryption", text: "TLS 1.2 in transit" },
  "The service is not region-locked",
]} />
```

- `ordered` for click-path procedures ("1. Navigate to All > AI Agent Studio").
- Bullets sit at 22px indent; keep items to a single line where possible.
