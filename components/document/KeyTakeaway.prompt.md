Ends a chapter. Place one after every major section, immediately before the chapter transition rule.

```jsx
<KeyTakeaway items={[
  "Priority is never manually set.",
  "Impact plus urgency determine priority.",
]} />
```

Two or three statements, each a full sentence under about twelve words, written as facts the
reader must carry forward. Not a summary of the chapter and not a list of topics covered.
Never two of these on the same page.
