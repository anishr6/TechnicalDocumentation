Teal, light-weight (300) heading used for every level of a technical document; never bold, never black.

```jsx
<SectionHeading level={2}>Action Required</SectionHeading>
<SectionHeading level={3}>Global Data Routing</SectionHeading>
```

- `level={2}` (32px) starts a page section and carries 44px of space above it.
- `level={3}` (22px) is the heading *inside* a DocCard or StepCard.
- Body copy under a heading stays `--text-body` — do not tint paragraphs teal.
