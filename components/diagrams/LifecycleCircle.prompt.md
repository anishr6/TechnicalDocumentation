Only for genuinely cyclical processes — the last stage must lead back into the first. A process that ends is a Timeline.

```jsx
<LifecycleCircle center="Incident lifecycle" stages={["New", "In Progress", "On Hold", "Resolved", "Closed"]} />
```

Four to six stages; stage names are one or two words. Raise `size` to 520 when stages carry
`text`. Always inside a `Figure`.
